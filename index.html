Methodology

Definitions, Assumptions & Caveats

Key Definitions

Win Rate: Won ÷ (Won + Lost). Active pipeline excluded. Standard CRM definition — most accurate read on completed sales cycles.

ARR Efficiency: Won ARR ÷ Total Opps Generated by channel, including active and lost. Captures the true cost of pipeline generation, not just closed deals.

Pipeline Coverage: Active Pipeline ARR ÷ Remaining ARR to Target. 3x is the healthy B2B SaaS benchmark. Below 1x is structurally insufficient.

Deal Cycle: Created Date to Close Date. Won = actual. Active = projected. Overall average: 353 days.

Important Caveats

📊 Sample sizes: Channel win rates are based on 9–22 closed deals. Directional confidence is high for 40+ point gaps; formal significance requires 3–4x more observations.

🔗 Single-touch attribution: One lead source per opportunity. Multi-touch modeling would change channel rankings.

💰 No spend data: ARR per opp is a proxy for efficiency. True ROAS and CAC require budget data not in this dataset.

🧪 Synthetic dataset: Directionally realistic by design. Real-world data would include missing values, duplicate opportunities, and multi-year history.
